Underwriting

Renewal Reversal

A 150-employee company came to us facing a 9.5% medical renewal increase. In a market where healthcare trend was running nearly 12%, many would have considered that a favorable outcome. Our client did not. To them, a 9.5% increase was still unacceptable.

The plan was structured as a POS arrangement, and the incumbent carrier would not release claims experience data. Rather than accept the renewal at face value, Acorn Benefits strategically positioned the group to competing carriers as a well-performing risk based on the below-trend increase.

Read more

Initial market responses were encouraging — but not enough. We continued negotiating aggressively with competing carriers while leveraging competitive intelligence with the incumbent carrier, who recognized the real possibility of losing the account.

The result? An 8% reduction from current rates. Not just a lower-than-expected increase — an actual decrease in cost.

The client secured a renewal outcome well beyond expectations, while the carrier retained a valued customer. Both parties walked away feeling successful. Even we were surprised by how far strategic negotiation and market leverage could move the pricing. This is the difference between simply accepting a renewal and actively managing one.

Claims Advocacy

Complex Case. Clear Advocacy. Meaningful Outcome.

An employee’s child required a rare, final-stage leg-lengthening procedure at Nemours Children's Hospital in Delaware. The specialized surgeon was out-of-network, and the treatment fell outside standard plan guidelines. Previous medical opinions had even recommended amputation.

When traditional customer service channels stalled, Acorn Benefits escalated the case — submitting detailed clinical documentation and working directly with the carrier’s medical director to secure an exception.

Read more

As a result of our advocacy, the carrier approved the surgeon and related services as in-network and extended physical therapy benefits beyond standard contractual limits to support the patient’s five-month recovery process.

This case reflects Acorn Benefits’ commitment to proactive claims advocacy, personalized client support, and navigating complex benefit challenges that materially impact employees and their families.

Advocacy

Expert Advocacy When It Matters

When an employee’s spouse was diagnosed with both breast cancer and colon cancer simultaneously, the focus was on treatment and recovery. After extensive care, her prognosis was positive. Four years later, however, a PET scan revealed new concerns, and the family once again faced difficult decisions.

The employee contacted Acorn Benefits after meeting with a nationally recognized breast cancer specialist who required payment in full upfront. We requested procedure codes and fees, then asked multiple insurance carriers and third-party administrators to independently review reimbursement comparisons based on usual, reasonable, and customary charges.

Read more

Our analysis revealed the specialist’s charges were nearly four times higher than prevailing amounts, with procedures extensively unbundled. To further protect the family, we consulted surgeons we trust and asked independent physicians in the insured’s area who they would recommend if it were their own spouse needing breast cancer surgery.

Armed with objective financial analysis and trusted medical guidance, the employee and his wife were able to make an informed decision during one of the most stressful moments of their lives.

This is what advocacy looks like when healthcare decisions become deeply personal.
