Renewal Reversal

A 150-employee company came to us facing a 9.5% medical renewal increase. In a market where healthcare trend was running nearly 12%, many would have considered that a favorable outcome. Our client did not. To them, a 9.5% increase was still unacceptable.

The plan was structured as a POS arrangement, and the incumbent carrier would not release claims experience data. Rather than accept the renewal at face value, Acorn Benefits strategically positioned the group to competing carriers as a well-performing risk based on the below-trend increase.

Read more

Initial market responses were encouraging — but not enough. We continued negotiating aggressively with competing carriers while leveraging competitive intelligence with the incumbent carrier, who recognized the real possibility of losing the account.

The result? An 8% reduction from current rates.

Not just a lower-than-expected increase — an actual decrease in cost. The client secured a renewal outcome well beyond expectations, while the carrier retained a valued customer. Both parties walked away feeling successful. Even we were surprised by how far strategic negotiation and market leverage could move the pricing. This is the difference between simply accepting a renewal and actively managing one.

Claims Advocacy

Complex Case. Clear Advocacy.

When an employee’s daughter required a rare and highly specialized leg-lengthening procedure, the family faced a difficult challenge. Several major hospitals had previously recommended amputation and a prosthetic, but a specialist at Alfred I. duPont Hospital in Delaware successfully pursued an alternative treatment plan over multiple surgeries.

By the time the final procedure was needed, the surgeon was considered out-of-network under the family’s current insurance plan. Because the treatment fell outside standard coverage guidelines, early attempts to secure approval were unsuccessful.

Read more

Acorn Benefits stepped in to advocate on the family’s behalf. Our team worked directly with the carrier’s representatives and medical director, providing detailed medical documentation and requesting specialized case management support. Due to the surgeon’s unique expertise and the complexity of the case, we successfully secured in-network coverage for the procedure and extended physical therapy support throughout the child’s five-month recovery process.

At Acorn Benefits, we believe employee benefits are about more than policies and paperwork. They are about people, families, and helping clients navigate life’s most challenging moments with expertise, persistence, and compassion.

Preserving Premium Benefits While Controlling Long-Term Costs

An investment firm client maintained a rich, high-end medical plan as part of its broader strategy to attract and retain top talent. Competitive benefits and compensation were considered essential to the firm’s culture and recruiting strategy.

However, the cost of maintaining this premium plan design was increasing rapidly and becoming financially unsustainable.

Acorn Benefits worked within evolving benefits legislation and conducted an extensive evaluation of carrier plan portfolios to develop an alternative funding strategy that preserved the client’s benefits philosophy while dramatically improving cost efficiency.

Read more

The Solution

The solution combined:

  • A strategically designed Health Reimbursement Arrangement (HRA)
  • A carefully selected carrier medical plan
  • A long-term risk management approach focused on sustainability

The Result

  • Reduced overall medical plan costs by approximately 40%
  • Maintained the high-end employee benefits experience the client valued
  • Preserved the company’s ability to attract and retain top talent
  • Created a sustainable long-term funding strategy

Most importantly, this approach continues to perform. Now in its sixth year, the program still costs less than the carrier renewal proposal originally presented when Acorn Benefits first introduced the concept.
