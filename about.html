About Us

Steady guidance for complex decisions.

Founded on the principle that trust is the foundation of effective consulting, Acorn Benefits has served business owners, CFOs, and HR leaders with clarity, integrity, and unwavering advocacy.

Our Story

Acorn Benefits was founded with a simple mission: to bring clarity and confidence to the complex world of employee benefits. After decades of experience on both the carrier and broker sides of the industry, we recognized a gap in the market for consulting that prioritized long-term partnership over transactional relationships.

The acorn symbolizes our approach—small, steady investments in trust and expertise that grow into lasting security for your organization. Like the oak tree that grows from humble beginnings, we believe that sound benefits strategy starts with solid foundations and patient cultivation.

Today, we serve businesses across the Pacific Northwest and beyond, bringing 60+ years of combined expertise to every client relationship.

Meet Our Founder

Meet Our Founder

Terry Brophy joined his friend and mentor, Joseph J. Oakes III, in developing the employee benefits division of Acorn Financial Services, now known as Acorn Benefits, LLC. Under their leadership, the firm has experienced significant and steady growth, built on a foundation of long-term client relationships, trust, and service. Terry often describes the company’s success as “controlled growth,” emphasizing client advocacy over sales metrics. He is more likely to share stories about helping clients navigate serious illnesses through claims intervention or negotiating difficult renewals than discussing production numbers.

Terry’s service philosophy began long before his insurance career. Growing up at the Jersey Shore, he worked as a busboy, valet attendant, waiter, and in other hospitality roles where exceptional service directly impacted success. Those experiences shaped his belief that professionalism, responsiveness, and genuine care are always rewarded.

Before joining Acorn, Terry built a distinguished career with Metropolitan Life, serving as a Group Sales Representative, Managing Group Sales Representative, and Senior Account Executive. He worked with organizations ranging from small businesses to employers with more than 10,000 employees, including nationally recognized law firms, hospitality organizations, and large corporate clients. His experience included implementing innovative self-insured health plan strategies and consulting on complex employee benefit programs.

Terry’s background combines financial, technical, and personal perspectives that allow him to fully understand the challenges employers and employees face. A graduate of Mount Saint Mary’s College with a degree in Accounting and a minor in English, he also earned his CPA designation. His accounting and underwriting knowledge provide a strong analytical foundation, while his communication skills help clients understand complex benefit decisions. Personal experiences, including caring for family members with serious medical conditions, reinforced for Terry the importance of strong employee benefits and compassionate advocacy.

At Acorn Benefits, Terry and his team focus on listening first — understanding each client’s goals, concerns, and culture — and then delivering thoughtful solutions that meet or exceed expectations.
